Anthropic priced its initial public offering of Class A common stock at $83.00 per share. The offering consists of 980,000,000 shares of Class A common stock. Of those, 956,351,354 are being sold by Anthropic and 23,648,646 are being sold by certain selling stockholders. Both portions of the offering are Class A common stock.

## Trading and timing

The shares are expected to begin trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on November 19, 2026 under the symbol ANT. Anthropic has granted the underwriters a 30-day option to purchase up to an additional 147,000,000 shares of its Class A common stock. The offering is expected to close on November 20, 2026, subject to the satisfaction of customary closing conditions. The trading start and the closing date are expectations, not completed events.

## Why it matters

For teams that buy model access, a public listing changes what the vendor has to file and publish. A registration statement relating to these securities was declared effective by the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) on November 18, 2026. That places the offering terms in a public filing, where buyers of the company's services can read them.
