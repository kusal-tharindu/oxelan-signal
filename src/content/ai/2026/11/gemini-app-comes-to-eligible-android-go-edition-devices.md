Google is bringing the Gemini app to eligible Android (Go edition) devices, according to the company’s announcement. The company re-architected the app so the core Gemini experience runs on hardware with less memory. The app can be downloaded on Google Play, the store Google names for getting started.

Gemini Live supports more than 45 languages. Google says the app will be preloaded on upcoming devices from partners like Transsion. The announcement frames Android (Go edition) as the platform for this expansion.

## Why it matters

The change puts the core Gemini experience on hardware with less memory, according to Google’s description of the re-architecture. For teams and users tracking AI app availability, the announcement adds Android (Go edition) to the list of eligible devices. The preload plan covers upcoming devices from partners like Transsion. The app remains available through Google Play.
