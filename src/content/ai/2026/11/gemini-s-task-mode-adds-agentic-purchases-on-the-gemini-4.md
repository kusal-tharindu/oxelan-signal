Google is rolling out a Gemini tasks update free of charge for hundreds of millions of people across the U.S., Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India. The update runs on the new Gemini 4 Hydrogen model. Gemini now uses the Agent Payments Protocol (AP2) to make purchases. Google says it is working with American Express (coming soon), Mastercard, and Visa to keep purchases secure with digital tokens on supported cards.

## Why it matters

Gemini now uses AP2 to make purchases, which puts the isolation and filtering around the task environment on the review list. Google says that environment is Google Cloud's Agent Compute Engine, a hardware-isolated virtual machine that is dedicated to the user's account and never shared with other users. The listed defenses include prompt-injection filters that scan search results, webpages, and more, alongside Google Safe Browsing. Gemini connects with more than 60 popular apps, and Google Wallet is expanding to iOS.

## What to do

Open Gemini, flip the home-screen toggle from Chat to Task, and tell Gemini what you need done. Starting in the U.S., users can also forward emails to gemini@google.com. Google asks researchers to submit findings through its bug bounty program, the AI Vulnerability Reward Program (AI VRP). The company says it will integrate the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) into Gemini next.
