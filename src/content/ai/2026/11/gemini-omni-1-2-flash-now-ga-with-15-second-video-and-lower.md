Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash is generally available on Google Cloud. It can generate and edit videos up to 15 seconds, up from 10 seconds with Omni 1.1. The model is priced at $0.07 per second of 720p video, 30% less than Omni 1.1 Flash. It supports duration in one-second increments from 3 to 15 seconds, can draft at 360p or 720p and upscale to 1080p or 4K, and outputs carry an imperceptible SynthID watermark.

Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash is available across the Gemini API and Gemini for business. It is also in Google Vids, where 15-second clips are generated at no additional cost with a Google account or Google Workspace subscription. The model is rolling out to the Gemini app for Google AI Ultra subscribers. Audio references are gated behind allowlisting. The release introduces multi-turn conversational editing and is backed by Google Cloud enterprise-grade infrastructure with Provisioned Throughput available.

The price drop to $0.07 per second and 30% reduction from Omni 1.1 Flash lower video generation cost. Drafting at 360p or 720p and upscaling to 1080p or 4K can speed iteration. SynthID watermarking helps with content provenance. Enterprise infrastructure and Provisioned Throughput support production use.

## What to do

Review the model for video workflows, using the documented 15-second limit and one-second increments as evaluation constraints. If your use case needs audio references, plan for allowlist approval. For production capacity, evaluate Provisioned Throughput.
