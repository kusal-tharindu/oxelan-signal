MiniMax released H3-Image, an open-weight image generation model built on H3. It supports text-to-image generation, reference-based generation, and image editing, and MiniMax says it generates a full-resolution 4K (4096 × 4096) image in under 10 seconds, at up to 5× the speed of mainstream alternatives.

## What changed

As with H3, MiniMax is releasing H3-Image's model weights. The model can work with up to 16 reference images, and MiniMax describes its prompt adherence as state-of-the-art among open-weight models. MiniMax reports that Standard mode is 1.5–2× as fast as mainstream alternatives, while Highspeed mode reaches 3–4× the speed. Pricing is 50–70% of comparable mainstream offerings.

## Why it matters

MiniMax reports 230+ countries and regions served, 300M+ global individual users, and 2M+ enterprise clients and developers. It offers AI-native products including MiniMax Code and MiniMax Design, alongside an open platform for enterprises and developers. For teams already generating images at 4K, the speed and price claims are the ones worth verifying first.

## What to do

H3-Image is available through the MiniMax API Platform, MiniMax Code, and Hailuo AI. For launch week, MiniMax is offering 50% off the standard price for image generation requests at 4K resolution and above.
