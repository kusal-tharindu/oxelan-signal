Le Groupe Figaro and OpenAI announced a strategic partnership that brings Le Figaro journalism into ChatGPT. The partnership covers current reporting and archive content from Le Figaro and associated publications, spanning news, business, culture and lifestyle.

In ChatGPT, Le Figaro articles will be discoverable using summaries and excerpts that include links to the underlying stories. Le Figaro says it will retain full editorial independence and control over its journalism.

## Why it matters

The arrangement puts a newspaper's current reporting and archive content inside an AI assistant, where readers meet the work as a summary or excerpt with a link back to the story. Groupe Figaro has a team of more than 600 journalists, and the agreement covers both current reporting and archive content. Le Figaro also plans to introduce ChatGPT Enterprise across various teams to support their day-to-day work.

## What to do

For teams weighing a similar arrangement, start with scope: which publications and which content types the deal covers. Check how the assistant presents the material, which here means summaries and excerpts with links to the original stories. Confirm the editorial-control terms, since Le Figaro states that it keeps full editorial independence and control over its journalism. If internal adoption is part of the plan, note that Le Figaro intends to roll out ChatGPT Enterprise across various teams.
