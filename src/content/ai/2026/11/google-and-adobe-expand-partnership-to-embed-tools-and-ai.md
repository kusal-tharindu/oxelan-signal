Google said at Adobe MAX that it is expanding its partnership with Adobe. The partnership will bring Adobe's creative and productivity tools directly into Google products, and it will bring Google's frontier artificial intelligence (AI) models into Adobe's apps.

## What is coming

Google Drive and Gmail will offer a direct pathway into Adobe Acrobat, surfaced as a suggested app across both services with an "Edit with" entry point in file previews. Adobe is expanding its connectors in Gemini and Gemini Enterprise with Acrobat and additional creative tools, and it will bring its creative tools to AI Mode in Google Search.

Businesses will be able to use Adobe's CX Enterprise Coworker agent in Gemini Enterprise. Adobe and Google DeepMind will explore new editing capabilities for creative professionals, starting with an image model for Photoshop that could enable near-real-time edits.

## Why it matters

The partnership puts Adobe's document and creative workflows inside Google products that teams already use, and it puts Google's models inside Adobe's apps. The CX Enterprise Coworker agent adds a business-facing agent to Gemini Enterprise, where it will sit alongside Adobe's connectors.
