Google has made Gemini 4 Hydrogen available globally in Google Search for all users, and Google describes the release as the biggest model upgrade to Search yet. The company says Hydrogen improves how Search understands intent, including nuanced and multi-part questions.

Hydrogen's reasoning also supports Search's query fan-out technique, which Google says the model supercharges. Google says the upgrade produces more visual responses. Those responses include images, structured responses, comparison tables, and custom tools.

It also provides richer language understanding across all supported AI Mode languages. For search users, the change affects how complex questions are interpreted and how answers are presented. Google's description emphasizes both understanding the user's request and assembling the final answer from the listed response types. The update spans intent, reasoning, visual presentation, and language support.
