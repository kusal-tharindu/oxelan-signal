According to SecurityWeek, the Pentagon renamed its Chief Digital and Artificial Intelligence Office (CDAO) the Super Intelligence Office. Cameron Stanley continues to lead the office as Chief Super Intelligence Officer for the Department of War, with Andrew Mapes serving as Principal Deputy Super Intelligence Officer.

The renaming follows Executive Order 14434, issued in September, which directed agencies to use "Super Intelligence" and "SI" in official communications. According to the report, the Department of War moved the office's public resources, procurement information and partnership portals to the SI.mil domain.

## What the office covers

The office's portfolio includes the Maven Smart System, an AI-enabled intelligence and targeting platform, and the War Data Platform, a central hub for military data.

## The wider renaming push

In October, Trump announced the "Super Intelligence Force," a government-wide AI coordination group chaired by Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton. The Justice Department announced its own Super Intelligence Task Force on November 5.

## Why it matters

Teams that follow US government AI programs now find the office's public material under a new name and a new domain. Documents, partner lists and procurement pages that still use the old name or domain no longer match the office's current naming.

## What to do

Organizations that reference the office should update the name and the SI.mil domain in their own documents and partner lists. The office's programs, including the Maven Smart System and the War Data Platform, are described in the same report under the new name.
