The new Project Genie experience includes first-time access to Genie 4. Google describes Genie 4 as its most realistic and interactive world model yet, and the release adds mid-session prompting: type a new prompt while navigating a world and the environment is edited in real time. Genie 4 also pairs its visuals with real-time native audio that adapts as the scene unfolds, from ambient soundscapes to character dialogue.

Genie 3 receives upgrades in the same release, with extended sessions that allow exploration for up to 3 minutes per generation, up from 60 seconds. New worlds can be seeded from 360-degree panorama images, and in-world events can be changed with natural-language prompts while you explore.

## Why it matters

The Project Genie agent orchestrates Google's multimodal models to craft initial visuals and custom background scores from a natural-language description. Curation comes with the same release: a new Project Genie gallery brings together curated worlds alongside creative work from the community. Generated video and audio in Project Genie carry SynthID watermarking.

## What to do

Eligible Google AI Ultra subscribers can try the new Project Genie experience starting today, and Google AI Pro members can join the waitlist on the same page.
