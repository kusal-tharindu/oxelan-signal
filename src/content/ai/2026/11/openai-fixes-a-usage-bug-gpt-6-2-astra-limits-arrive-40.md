According to BleepingComputer, OpenAI confirmed that it fixed a bug that incorrectly discounted GPT-6.2 usage. After the correction, users will reach their limits 40% faster on GPT-6.2 Astra.

## What changed

Usage of GPT-6.2 Astra had been counted at a discount, and OpenAI has now corrected that accounting. The change affects GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol usage, while other models retain their existing usage calculations. The report attributes the confirmation to Tibo Sottiaux, who works on Codex and ChatGPT at OpenAI.

## Why it matters

For anyone running GPT-6.2 Astra against a plan allowance, the same work now consumes that allowance faster than before. Planning that assumed the old rate will run out sooner, and that applies to GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol alike.

## What to do

OpenAI said it will not charge users for the usage difference caused by the mistake. It is adding one banked usage reset for everyone on the Go, Plus, Pro, and Business plans. The report says a new reset has been added to ChatGPT, and that it expires a month later.
