## What changed

Google is rolling out 66 new regional voices in the Gemini app on Android and iOS. The set of new voices includes several English variants alongside Hindi, Brazilian Portuguese, Indonesian, and Korean. Google had shared plans at its Google I/O event to bring local dialects to Gemini.

## Why it matters

Gemini Live is the app's spoken conversation mode, and the Listen icon reads chat replies aloud. A voice selected in the app carries across the entire app, including both of those surfaces, so a single choice covers spoken conversation and read-aloud replies.

## What to do

Users can preview the new voices from the three-dot menu in Gemini Live. The same preview option is available in Gemini's voice settings in the app. Previewing from either place lets a user hear a regional voice and compare it before settling on one. Once chosen, that voice is what the app uses in Gemini Live and behind the Listen icon in chat.
