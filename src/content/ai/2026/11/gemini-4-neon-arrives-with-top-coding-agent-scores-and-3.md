## What changed

Gemini 4 Neon is rolling out in the Gemini API in Google AI Studio. The model has a 1 million-token context window and is priced at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens.

## Benchmarks

On the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, Gemini 4 Neon scored 61 at its xhigh thinking level, third overall. It ranks second on the Vals Index at 70.9%, debuts second globally with a score of 1336 on Arena's Agent Arena Index, and scores 73.5% on Chartography for reading dense professional charts. It reaches first place on both the Legal Research Bench and the Tax Agent Bench.

For coding and agent work, Gemini 4 Neon ranks first overall on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index at 58.6 and scores 65.2% on Terminal-Bench 4.1. At its default high setting it reaches 46.2% on FrontierCode 2.0, is best in class on Terminal-Bench Science at 70.5%, and scores 84.0% on RiemannBench and 71.3% on HLE-Diamond. It also achieves state-of-the-art results on the hard tier of AutoBaxBench at 76%.

## Security and safety evaluations

Gemini 4 Neon is state-of-the-art on WebExploitBench v1.2 at 51.26%, achieves state-of-the-art performance on SEC-bench Pro at 90.5%, and reaches 70.7% on Collinear's CWE-bench v2. On the safety side, it reports a 0.09% misaligned action rate on the agentic safety suite and a 99.2% pass rate on the agent honesty evaluation. It refused 1.98% of tasks on Terminal-Bench 4.0 and resisted every attack within the Gray Swan IPI benchmark at its highest thinking level with real-time safeguards.

## Availability

Gemini 4 Neon is available in the Gemini API in Google AI Studio. Gemini 4 Neon for cyber is rolling out to trusted defenders in the coming days as part of the Fairwind Program.
