Gemini 4 Hydrogen is available to developers starting today. The model runs standalone across four configurable thinking levels, and it can run alongside frontier models such as Gemini 4 Carbon and Gemini 4 Argon.

## What it is for

Hydrogen is designed for repository coding and debugging, interactive web development, and multimodal workflows. In plain terms, that is work inside an existing repository, an interactive web build, and tasks that combine more than one type of input.

## Why it matters

Hydrogen can run alongside frontier models such as Gemini 4 Carbon and Gemini 4 Argon, so a team can use it together with the models it already has. The four thinking levels are configurable rather than a single fixed setting.

## What to do

Developers can build with Hydrogen through the Gemini API (application programming interface, the interface a program uses to call the model) in Google AI Studio and Android Studio. Gemini for business is the other listed route, and paid Google AI Pro or Ultra subscribers can try the model in Google Antigravity. Access is open from today.
