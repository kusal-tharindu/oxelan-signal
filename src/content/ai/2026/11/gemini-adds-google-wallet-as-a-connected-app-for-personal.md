Google is adding Google Wallet to the list of connected apps in Gemini, so responses can be tailored around personal finance. All Gemini app users in the U.S. can connect Wallet to get answers informed by their spending habits. Google Wallet in Gemini is rolling out across web and mobile, for Gemini app users on Android and iOS.

Users can go further by linking financial accounts from credit cards and banks to Wallet through Plaid, which the announcement describes as an option. Gemini can then generate dynamic charts tracking cash flow, asset allocation, and budgets directly in the chat. It can also find recurring charges across linked accounts and flag price increases.

## Why it matters

Connecting Wallet changes which data Gemini's answers can draw on. Google states that with Wallet connected, Gemini can only read information from accounts and cannot move, manage, or invest money. Whether to make that connection is the user's choice, and it can be reversed.

## What to do

Open Connected Apps in Personal Intelligence settings to connect Wallet, and use the same place to turn the connection off at any time. In Gemini, tag @Google Wallet to ask a personal finance or budgeting question. Saved preferences, interests, and ongoing goals appear in Memory Summary. You can view, refine, or remove them via Profile > Personal Intelligence > Memory Summary.
