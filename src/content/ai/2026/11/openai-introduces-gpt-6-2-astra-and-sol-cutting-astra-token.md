OpenAI introduced GPT-6.2 Astra, a new frontier model for hard projects, and GPT-6.2 Sol, an efficient, versatile model for everyday work. Astra's input and output token prices are 20% lower than GPT-6 Astra's, while Sol keeps GPT-6.1 Sol's API token prices.

## Benchmarks

On Agents' Last Exam, GPT-6.2 Astra scored 5.2 points higher than GPT-6 Astra at less than a third of the cost. On the same benchmark it scored 5.5 points ahead of Claude Fable 5.2 at just 5% of the cost. GPT-6.2 Astra also scores 9 percentage points higher than GPT-6 Astra on Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1 at less than one-third the cost.

On Harvey LAB-AA v1.1, GPT-6.2 Astra more than doubles Claude Fable 5.2's pass rate at about one-sixth the cost. On Artificial Analysis's AA-3D, GPT-6.2 Astra ranks first with 1,318 Elo, followed by Claude Fable 5.2 at 1,271 and GPT-6.2 Sol at 1,259. On AA-AnalystAgent, GPT-6.2 Astra answers 62.5% of questions correctly on all five independent attempts.

## Pricing and the smaller model

GPT-6.2 Astra's API (application programming interface) pricing is $8 per million input tokens, $0.40 per million cached input tokens, and $40 per million output tokens. In Cognition's FrontierCode 2.0, GPT-6.2 Sol comes within 0.5 points of GPT-6.2 Astra at about one-fifth the cost. On LifeVal, GPT-6.2 Sol outscores GPT-6 Astra at about one-seventh the cost.

On internal GDPval v2, GPT-6.2 Astra is up 6.2 percentage points from GPT-6 Astra while using 22% fewer output tokens. GPT-6.2 Sol improves 5.3 points from GPT-6.1 Sol on that benchmark.

## Safety posture

Both models meet the Critical threshold for cybersecurity under OpenAI's Preparedness Framework. OpenAI says it is launching GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol with tighter safeguards for higher-risk cybersecurity assistance. Through OpenAI Daybreak, defenders can currently access GPT-6 Sol with reduced cyber refusals on Daybreak Blue, and OpenAI will work to bring the GPT-6.2 models to Daybreak.
