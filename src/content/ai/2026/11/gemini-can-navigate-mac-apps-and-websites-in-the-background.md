Gemini can now navigate Mac apps and websites in the background to finish complex tasks. The capability is called computer use, and it is part of the Gemini app for Mac, where it helps users get multi-step tasks done across local files, desktop apps, and the web.

Gemini works inside an isolated background window, and the chat shows a live preview of what it is doing. Users choose which apps Gemini can access. They can watch every step live, pause at any time, and approve key actions before those actions happen.

Computer use is rolling out in English in the U.S. in the Gemini app for Mac. The app can be downloaded at gemini.google/mac.

## Why it matters

The controls let the user decide which apps Gemini can access and which key actions need approval. The app also shows a live preview of each step in the chat.

## What to do

Download the Gemini app for Mac at gemini.google/mac.
