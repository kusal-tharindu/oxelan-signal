## What changed

Google introduced Gemini 4 Hydrogen on November 12, 2026. The model has a 1 million-token context window and is priced at $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens. Google says it finishes tasks 2.5 times faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash at its high default setting. It has four thinking levels: low, medium, high as the default, and xhigh.

## Benchmarks and agent behavior

At its default high thinking level, Gemini 4 Hydrogen scores 51 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, versus 50 for Argon, and it reaches 54 at xhigh. On Terminal-Bench 4.1 it scores 57% versus 18.2% for Gemini 3.8 Flash. Google reports that it reduces overreach in agentic tasks by up to 81% and cuts misaligned actions and task shortcutting across tool-use and coding evaluations by 12x. The model ranks #5 on Agent Arena and scores 71.6% on Agents' Last Exam v1.1. Google reports a 96.7% honesty rate on its internal agent honesty evaluation.

## Availability

Gemini 4 Hydrogen is available across all tiers of Google AI Studio, the Gemini API, Gemini for business, the Gemini app, Google Search, and for paid Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in Google Antigravity. Google also introduced Orchestrator in Google Antigravity, which switches between models such as Carbon and Hydrogen within the same session. On Terminal-Bench 4.0, Orchestrator reached near-Carbon task success rates while consuming 24% less quota and completing 19% faster.
