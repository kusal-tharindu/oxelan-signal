LVMH Group's Media Division and OpenAI announced on November 16, 2026 that they had signed a strategic agreement in Paris. The announcement covers both the tools the division's teams will use and how the division's articles appear in ChatGPT.

## What the agreement covers

The agreement includes the deployment of OpenAI tools and ChatGPT Enterprise to teams across the media division. According to the announcement, the partnership also enhances the presence of the division's content in ChatGPT, providing summaries and excerpts in response to relevant user questions, identifying their sources and including direct links to the original articles.

## The division behind the deal

Groupe Les Echos-Le Parisien, the media division of LVMH Group, brings together 17 media brands covering news, finance, culture, heritage, arts and music. It has 1,800 employees, including nearly 700 journalists, and reaches more than 27 million people in France every month.

## Why it matters

The announcement covers both sides of an AI relationship for a publisher: tools deployed to the division's teams, and the division's articles surfaced in ChatGPT with source identification and links to the originals. Michèle Benbunan, CEO of LVMH Group's Media Division, and Emmanuel Marill, Managing Director, EMEA at OpenAI, are named in the announcement.
