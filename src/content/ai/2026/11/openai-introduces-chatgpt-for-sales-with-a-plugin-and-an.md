OpenAI introduced ChatGPT for Sales to help sales teams understand customers better, spend less time on busywork, and close more deals. The product brings tools, customer context, and sales workflows into one place and includes a new Sales plugin with dedicated skills for sellers. It also includes more than 30 new and enhanced partner plugins in ChatGPT.

## What is included

The Sales plugin gives ready-to-use workflows for everyday sales work, from deciding which accounts to pursue to preparing for a call and drafting the follow-up. Users can delegate work to their dot, an always-on agent in ChatGPT that can use the same plugins and customer context. Partner plugins cover learning sales processes and product updates with Confluence, Guru, and Seismic, building tailored presentations with ChatGPT Slides, Gamma, Figma, or Canva, and bringing together account context from Salesforce, HubSpot, Close CRM (customer relationship management), or monday CRM. They also combine account signals from Apollo, Clay, and Common Room with ChatGPT's web research, plan outreach with Outreach or Salesloft, use call notes from the Meetings plugin in ChatGPT, Gong, Otter.ai, or Granola, use signals from Backstory to identify deals that need attention, and prepare quotes and agreements with Docusign, Ironclad, PandaDoc, or ServiceNow CPQ (configure, price, quote).

## Why it matters in production

The available partner plugins and features depend on the plan, region, and workspace settings, so the effective feature set can differ between workspaces.

## What to do

ChatGPT Business and Enterprise admins can enable ChatGPT for Sales for their team by opening the Admin Console, selecting the workspace, and selecting Plugins. Confirm which partner plugins and features your plan, region, and workspace settings support before you enable the product.
