Gemini 4 Carbon was made available to developers, enterprises, scientists, and consumers on November 10, 2026. The model is available through the API (application programming interface) in Google AI Studio and Gemini for business for all paying developers and enterprises.

## Availability

Gemini 4 Carbon is rolling out to all Google AI Ultra subscribers in Google Antigravity, the Gemini app, and AI Mode in Search. It is priced at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, the same introductory price as Gemini 4 Argon.

## Why it matters

The release adds another model for paying developers and enterprises at the Argon introductory price. Gemini 4 Carbon ranks #1 on CursorBench 4.0, Perplexity's WANDR, and Zapier's AutomationBench. In Project Fortify, Gemini 4 Carbon hit 100% recall on scanning and 95% precision on triage on Google's internal benchmark.

## Security use

Two weeks before general availability, Google released Carbon to a set of cybersecurity defenders via its Fairwind Program. Google's Cloud Vulnerability Research team used Carbon to prove two bugs in a popular open-source software were exploitable vulnerabilities.

## What to do

Developers can build with Gemini 4 Carbon through the paid API tier in Google AI Studio and Android Studio.
