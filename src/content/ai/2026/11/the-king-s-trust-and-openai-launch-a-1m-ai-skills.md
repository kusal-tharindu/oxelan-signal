The King's Trust has announced a major partnership with OpenAI to help young people build AI skills for work. The program aims to support up to 25,000 young people over the next 18 months. OpenAI is making a £1m donation to the youth charity to support the work.

## Who the program targets

The partnership prioritizes young people not in education, employment or training (NEET). The announcement cites the Open University's Business Barometer 2026, which found that 68% of surveyed 16–24-year-olds who were not in education, employment or training would be willing to train or upskill to improve their job prospects.

## What comes next

The King's Trust will launch a wider program of nationally recognized AI qualifications in the coming months. The announcement also points to OpenAI Academy, which provides free learning resources for students, job-seekers and other learners.

## Context

The announcement comes as The King's Trust marks its 50th year. The charity describes a 50-year legacy of supporting more than 1.3 million young people.
