OpenAI announced advances in computer-use agents for healthcare that let AI agents carry out multi-step work across existing healthcare software. The company also introduced more than 20 new plugins to its healthcare plugin ecosystem. Computer-use agent capability is available in the OpenAI API, ChatGPT Work, and Codex.

In a blind evaluation with over 1300 reviews, physicians rated the updated clinical search experience more highly across all eight dimensions evaluated, according to OpenAI. Those dimensions included factual correctness, safety, actionability, and source authoritativeness. The plugins named in the announcement include Redox, Elation Health, K Health, Metriport, and UpToDate Expert AI. OpenAI is also working with healthcare organizations including Abridge, Ambience, Cigna Healthcare, and CVS Health to deploy agents.

## Why it matters

Because the agents perform multi-step work across existing healthcare software, the review covers the software they act in as well as the model behind them. Both ChatGPT for Healthcare and ChatGPT for Clinicians offer customers a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with OpenAI to support HIPAA-compliant workflows. Content shared with ChatGPT for Healthcare is not used to train models.

## What to do

Computer-use agents in ChatGPT Work and Codex, and the improved trusted clinical search, are available today for ChatGPT for Healthcare customers, subject to workspace settings. Review those workspace settings before you enable the agents. Confirm that the BAA covers your deployment if you need HIPAA-compliant workflows. All of the new plugins are available today in ChatGPT for Healthcare, including through ChartGPT, so check which ones you turn on. ChatGPT for Clinicians is free for eligible, verified clinicians in the U.S..
