According to SecurityWeek, Frontline Education suffered a data breach in August 2026 affecting more than 3 million people. Frontline detected the intrusion on August 14, 2026, and the last day of unauthorized activity was August 15, 2026. According to SecurityWeek, attackers gained access by exploiting a vulnerability in a third-party software product.

## What is affected

According to SecurityWeek, the hackers stole personal information of school district employees and students from two Frontline products, Frontline Central and School Health Management. Frontline has not named the exploited third-party product or the attacker, the report said. Frontline says the incident has been fully remediated.

## Why it matters

According to SecurityWeek, the compromised data may include names, Social Security numbers, dates of birth, driver's license numbers, passport and other government ID numbers, health insurance information such as Medicaid IDs, and student IDs. Frontline Education says it supports more than 10,000 K-12 organizations nationwide.

## What to do

According to SecurityWeek, affected adults in the US are offered two years of free credit monitoring and identity theft protection, and eligible minors are offered free dark web monitoring. Frontline began sending individual notification letters on or about October 26, 2026. The TransUnion call center can be reached at 1-800-759-7279.
