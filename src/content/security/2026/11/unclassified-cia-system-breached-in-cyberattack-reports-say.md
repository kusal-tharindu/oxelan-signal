According to SecurityWeek, an unclassified CIA system was breached in a cyberattack. The report says the intrusion affected a system that handles open source intelligence, that no classified information was involved, and that the issue has been resolved. The breach was first reported by The New York Times on Tuesday, according to the report.

According to BleepingComputer, unknown attackers reportedly breached an open-source intelligence (OSINT) analysis system at the CIA. That account says the cyberattack was reportedly carried out using the ARTEX AI-powered penetration testing framework. It adds that the breach reportedly affected the Office of the Director of National Intelligence (ODNI) and the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency, and that no classified data or systems were exposed.

## What is not known

According to SecurityWeek, it was not clear what specific information was compromised or who was responsible. SecurityWeek describes an unclassified system that handles open source intelligence, while BleepingComputer describes an OSINT analysis system at the CIA.

## Related activity

According to BleepingComputer, FinCEN disclosed that it is investigating a cybersecurity incident involving unauthorized access to a limited subset of its systems. The outlet also reported that the National Security Agency's (NSA) classified network suffered a days-long outage, which the agency blamed on scheduled routine maintenance.
