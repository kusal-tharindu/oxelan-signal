According to BleepingComputer, SBC Medical Group disclosed two cybersecurity incidents that exposed sensitive customer and employee information, including personal data of 1.6 million people. The report said the company discovered the database breach on November 16 and was notified of the Tribeau compromise the same day. SBC described the database incident as exposing basic personal information belonging to 1,624,000 people. The same incident exposed 255,416 reservation requests and 217,769 inquiries submitted to SBC websites.

## What was exposed

The report said attackers stole approximately 20,000 employee account records, including names, IDs, and passwords. For a subset of 459 employees, the affected data also included bank account and tax information. The report said the second incident involved administrator accounts on the Tribeau booking platform, a service used by 56 clinics. The report said the operator reported access to reservation information for 12,641 people, as well as treatment names, dates, and fees for 6,804 people who received treatment.

## Why it matters

SBC said it has not confirmed unauthorized access to electronic medical records or information from its mySBC reservation site in the database breach. As of November 18, when the Form 8-K was submitted, the company had not seen evidence of misuse of the exposed data.

## What to do

Because SBC said the investigation remains ongoing and it could not estimate the financial impact, the cited reporting does not describe a fixed version or a mitigation step. Readers should follow SBC Medical Group's SEC filing.
