According to BleepingComputer, the Gentlemen ransomware group has added NVIDIA to its dark web data leak site. The report says the group has not yet shared any proof of the breach.

According to the report, NVIDIA says it is investigating an incident that may have affected a third-party partner. The company also says it has found no indication that NVIDIA systems or networks were affected.

## Why it matters

The Gentlemen operates as a double-extortion group, exfiltrating sensitive data from victims' networks before encrypting their systems. According to the report, the group has claimed more than 1,100 victims from various sectors. It has also switched to a Ransomware-as-a-Service (RaaS) model, offering affiliates up to a 90% cut of ransom proceeds.

## NVIDIA has been claimed before

According to the report, NVIDIA confirmed a data breach in May that exposed some Armenian users' information after a regional GeForce NOW Alliance partner was hacked. The company also disclosed a breach in March 2022, claimed by the Lapsus$ extortion gang, that affected more than 71,000 employees.

## What to watch

The countdown on the group's leak site for NVIDIA ends on November 28. Until the group shares proof, the listing is a claim rather than a confirmed breach.
