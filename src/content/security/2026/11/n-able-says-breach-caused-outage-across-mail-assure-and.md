According to BleepingComputer, N-able says a security breach led to a major outage affecting Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud services worldwide, across the Americas, Asia-Pacific, and Europe. N-able said it has no evidence that customer data was compromised, including access to tenants' mailboxes.

## What is affected

The report said N-able took down the management portal, quarantine, logging, and mailbox synchronization supporting services after detecting the attack. The report also said the cyberattack did not affect email flow or spam filtering.

## Why it matters

The takedown affected supporting services rather than email flow, which N-able said was unaffected along with spam filtering. The report said the incident is unrelated to an N-central incident that forced N-able to take all hosted N-central environments offline to patch more than 30 zero-day flaws.

## What to do

N-able took the affected supporting services offline after detecting the attack, and the cited sources do not list fixed versions; follow the vendor's advisory for restoration and updates.
