Carla Magaly Alcedo Mendoza operated a Peruvian call center that defrauded and extorted U.S. residents by falsely threatening them with arrest, court proceedings and immigration consequences. She was sentenced to eight years in prison and three years of supervised release.

Alcedo and her co-conspirators impersonated government officials and lawyers to intimidate victims and force them to pay. The scheme defrauded more than 30,000 Spanish-speaking U.S. residents out of $15 million. She pleaded guilty to conspiracy to commit mail and wire fraud in August 2026. According to BleepingComputer, Alcedo is 45 years old and from Lima, Peru.

The report said she managed Peruvian call centers linked to fraud schemes between January 2013 and December 2018. She was extradited from Peru in April 2025. The report also said she was arrested in March 2023.

Fourteen defendants have now been convicted and sentenced in connection with the scheme. Alcedo is the 10th defendant charged in the scheme to be extradited from Peru and sentenced in federal court. The report also cited a warning from the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) that Americans lost $3.5 billion to impersonation scams in 2025. The report also said the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) found U.S. victims lost nearly $21 billion to cyber-enabled crimes in 2025.

## What to do

The sentencing is a law-enforcement outcome; no technical action is required.
