SBC Medical Group Holdings Incorporated reported cybersecurity incidents involving customer information associated with certain clinics in Japan. On November 16, 2026, unauthorized access occurred to certain databases within a website system managed and operated by SBC Medical Group, Ltd.. The company confirmed that an unauthorized third party accessed and extracted customer information.

According to SecurityWeek, the breach impacted over 2 million individuals in Japan. The outlet reported that hackers accessed an online platform SBC Medical Group manages and compromised databases containing customer information associated with certain clinics in Japan.

## What was taken

Customer passwords were obtained in the incident. Employee account information, including names, IDs and passwords, was also obtained for employees of the company's group and the medical institutions it supports, involving approximately 20,000 records. For 459 employees, employment-related information including bank account and pension-related information was affected. Credit card information for 14 individuals was included in affected inquiry form data, and those cards could not be used for additional payments.

## The separate Tribeau incident

On November 16, 2026, SBC Medical Group was notified of unauthorized access to clinic administrator accounts on Tribeau, a third-party reservation service used by 56 clinics. According to SecurityWeek, hackers accessed administrator accounts and the information of approximately 19,000 individuals in that separate incident.

## Response

SBC Medical Group isolated the affected server and blocked unauthorized access after detecting anomalous activity. The company reported the database incident to Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission on November 16. SecurityWeek reported that SBC rotated the passwords for all clinics it provides management services to on Tribeau.

## What to do

Clinics that use Tribeau should confirm that their administrator passwords were changed, as SecurityWeek reported that SBC rotated the passwords for all clinics it provides management services to on the service.
