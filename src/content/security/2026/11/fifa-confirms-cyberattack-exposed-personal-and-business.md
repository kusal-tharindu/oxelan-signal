According to SecurityWeek, FIFA was the victim of a cyberattack that resulted in unauthorized access to a limited set of personal and business information. FIFA said it is working closely with relevant authorities, including law enforcement.

The report also says a group calling itself the Handsome Boy Hacking Club posted a screenshot on X of what it claimed were FIFA files. Three days later, the group appeared to take credit for the hack when responding to a post that said more than 12 gigabytes of internal documents had been leaked. FIFA condemned the unlawful acquisition and publication of information, particularly personal data.

## Why it matters

The public claim about internal documents is broader than FIFA's confirmed account. FIFA did not say when the attack took place or the precise nature of the hacked information.
