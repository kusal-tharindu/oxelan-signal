According to SecurityWeek, the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) is investigating an apparent hack of a national law enforcement database it maintains. The report identifies the system as the National Crime Information Center (NCIC), a computerized database of criminal justice information available to local, state and federal law enforcement agencies. According to the report, the NCIC includes records on fugitives, missing persons, criminal history data, stolen property and other topics.

The report says the news network CNN first reported the apparent hack and that the FBI declined to comment on it. In a Fox News Channel interview about the incident, President Donald Trump said: "We just heard about it, and I will say that it's not good."

## Earlier incidents at the bureau

In September, the criminal hacking group ShinyHunters revealed it had obtained a massive trove of data on FBI employees and applicants and had compromised the bureau's jobs portal. In March, the FBI disclosed that it was investigating "suspicious activities" on an internal system containing sensitive information related to surveillance operations and investigations.

## What is not yet known

The report says the scope of the problem was not immediately clear. The cited report describes no patch, fixed version, or mitigation step for the database.
