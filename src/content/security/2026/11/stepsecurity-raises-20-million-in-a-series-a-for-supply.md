According to SecurityWeek, the supply chain security startup StepSecurity has raised $20 million in a Series A funding round. The report says the round was led by Elephant, with additional support from Runtime Ventures, Inner Loop Capital, and SaaS Ventures. The Series A brings the company's total raised to $23 million.

## What the platform covers

Founded in 2021, Seattle-based StepSecurity has built a platform that combines developer-machine protection, repository checks, a hosted upstream proxy and policy gate, and a CI/CD (continuous integration and continuous delivery) security agent. The company also offers a free community tier of its CI/CD runtime security agent Harden-Runner, which the report says is already used by over 20,000 open source projects. Harden-Runner now integrates a free Secure Registry proxy and policy gate tier, which the report says is meant to help open source maintainers block compromised packages.

## What the funding is for

According to the report, StepSecurity will use the funding to grow its engineering team and enhance its security research team. The report also says the company plans to expand go-to-market efforts.
