According to BleepingComputer, authorities arrested 354 suspects in a joint international operation targeting dark web marketplaces. The report says the action covered vendors and buyers on the Archetyp, Nemesis, Tor2Door, Bohemia, and Kingdom marketplaces.

## What the operation covered

The action was codenamed Operation TorNado and ran from May 2025 to October 2026, with agencies from 14 countries taking part, according to the report. Seizures in Europe included over €60 million ($66 million) in cash and cryptocurrency, 950 kilograms of illicit drugs and 57 firearms. In the United States, law enforcement seized more than 4.8 metric tons of illicit drugs, 152 firearms, and over €150 million ($166 million) in cash and cryptocurrency.

## Why it matters

The report frames the action around vendors and buyers on the named marketplaces, rather than around a single site or a software flaw. It also places the action in a line of similar enforcement operations stretching back to 2020, rather than treating it as a one-off takedown.

## Context

DisrupTor in 2020 led to 179 arrests, and Operation SpecTor in 2023 led to 288 arrests and the seizure of €50.8 million ($55.9 million) in cash and cryptocurrency, according to the report. In May 2025, Operation RapTor produced 270 arrests of dark web vendors and buyers from ten countries. Earlier, in April 2022, US authorities and German police took down Hydra, described in the report as the world's largest dark web marketplace at the time.
