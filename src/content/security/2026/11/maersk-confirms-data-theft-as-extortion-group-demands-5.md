According to SecurityWeek, Maersk confirmed that hackers extracted data from one of its IT systems. In a November 11 statement, the shipping group said the affected system was inherited through an earlier acquisition.

The report says an extortion group is demanding $5 million. It names WhiteArmor, which listed Maersk on its leak site on November 7 and claims to have obtained approximately 100GB of data from a platform associated with LF Logistics. Maersk acquired LF Logistics, a Hong Kong-based logistics company, in 2022.

WhiteArmor's extortion message gave 30 days to pay USD 5,000,000 in bitcoin. The group threatened to release the information if payment is not made by 7 December.

## Why it matters

The account so far comes from Maersk and from the group making the demand, and it leaves several questions open. Maersk said its findings so far strongly suggest no sensitive data has been disclosed, and that the incident is isolated to a local system. The group's claims have not been independently verified, and Maersk has not confirmed WhiteArmor's involvement. The company has also not disclosed when the intrusion occurred or how the attackers gained access.

## What to do

Maersk urged customers to exercise caution over unexpected communications referencing the incident. The cited sources describe a data-theft incident rather than a product flaw, and give no patch or fixed version to apply.
