## The extradition

Roman Khlynovskiy, 43, a Kazakh national, was extradited from Italy to the United States. He made his initial appearance in federal court in Knoxville, Tennessee, on Monday, November 16, 2026. Prosecutors allege Khlynovskiy was a member of the "8-Digits Team," which was affiliated with the INC and Lynx ransomware.

## The alleged operation

The alleged conspiracy targeted victims in at least six states, including both schools and hospitals. The conspirators boasted they possessed over 50 terabytes of data consisting of more than 70 million confidential health records. Their actions resulted in over $500 million in ransom demands.

## Charges and case status

Khlynovskiy is charged with conspiracy to commit computer fraud and identity theft, conspiracy to commit wire fraud, extortion involving computers, conspiracy to commit money laundering, and threats to disclose nonconsensual intimate images in violation of the TAKE IT DOWN Act. If convicted, he faces a maximum penalty of 27 years in prison.

According to BleepingComputer, INC Ransom emerged as a ransomware-as-a-service (RaaS) operation in July 2023. The report says Lynx ransomware surfaced in July 2024 and is believed to be a rebrand of INC ransomware.

The Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) is investigating the case, and the Justice Department's Office of International Affairs worked with the government of Italy to secure the arrest and extradition. Ukraine's State Bureau of Investigation and Prosecutor General's Office and Lithuanian authorities provided substantial assistance.
