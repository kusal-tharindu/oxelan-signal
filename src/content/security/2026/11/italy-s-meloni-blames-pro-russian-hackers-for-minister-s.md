According to SecurityWeek, Italian Foreign Minister Antonio Tajani said his personal data had been disseminated on the dark web. Tajani, who is also a vice premier in Italy's government, said cybercriminals have been spreading his personal data for days. He said he has been exposed to threats and intimidation and that he will not allow himself to be intimidated.

Italian Premier Giorgia Meloni blamed pro-Russian hackers for the leak. She expressed solidarity with Tajani following the revelations about the activities of pro-Russian hackers and the publication of personal data on the dark web. Tajani did not indicate who might be responsible for the cyberattack.

Meloni called attempts to sell confidential information about government officials a matter of grave concern. The report says the leak followed a string of hoax bomb threats across Europe that caused flight diversions.

## What is affected

No affected product, version or installation is named in the report; the exposed material is personal data.
