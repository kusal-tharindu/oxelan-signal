## What changed

According to The Hacker News, Switzerland's NCSC (National Cyber Security Centre) has confirmed the country's first cases in which AI agents were used in cyberattacks against companies and operators of critical infrastructure. In four of the five confirmed attacks, attackers using AI agents broke into computer systems at Swiss organizations.

Three of the attacks targeted critical infrastructure operators, and two of those succeeded, with no major disruptions reported. The Swiss advisory named ARTEX and CyberStrikeAI as examples of agents reachable over the internet, not as the tools used in the five cases.

## Wider context

Italy's CSIRT Italia (Computer Security Incident Response Team) described mass compromise campaigns in an official bulletin this week and named ARTEX, CyberStrikeAI, PentAGI, and DeepSeek Harness as tools used in those campaigns. The UK's NCSC, the report says, has seen a marked rise in AI-assisted cyber attacks since September 2026. Attackers using ARTEX broke into the European Commission's human resources, medical reimbursement, and translation systems, in an earlier intrusion first detected on October 22 that the Commission has confirmed.

## Why it matters

Two of the three attacks on critical infrastructure operators succeeded. The link to AI agents is a national agency's confirmation, and the same reporting connects that tooling to mass campaigns in Italy and to an intrusion at the European Commission.
