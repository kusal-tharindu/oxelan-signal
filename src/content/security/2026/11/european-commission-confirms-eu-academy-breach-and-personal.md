According to BleepingComputer, the European Commission confirmed that attackers breached its EU Academy online learning hub and stole personal data of users. The report said hackers breached its systems on October 22 using the ARTEX AI-powered penetration testing framework. The attack caused service disruptions that also affected the Commission's internal Sysper HR platform.

According to SecurityWeek, compromised information includes names and email addresses of EU Academy users. SecurityWeek also reported that the cyberattack was detected on October 22 and that the Commission said it had largely been contained.

## Why it matters

BleepingComputer reported that the Commission has not received a ransom demand and has yet to attribute the attack. SecurityWeek reported that access to EU Academy has been suspended during the investigation and that the European Data Protection Supervisor (EDPS) has been notified.

## What to do

The report said potentially affected individuals have not been informed directly; a general message was posted on the website. EU Academy users should check the platform's website for the Commission's notice; BleepingComputer reported that the notice says public access to the platform has been disabled.
