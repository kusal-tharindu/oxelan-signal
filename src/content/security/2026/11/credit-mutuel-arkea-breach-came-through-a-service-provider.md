According to BleepingComputer, Crédit Mutuel Arkéa was targeted in a cyberattack that enabled fraudulent transactions of up to $133 million. The report says the attacker reached the bank's network directly through a service provider's platform.

Reports about the incident emerged on November 2, initially from blockchain fraud investigator ZachXBT, and Crédit Mutuel Arkéa disclosed it on November 4. According to the report, the funds may have been withdrawn as USDC and USDT stablecoins, and the bank did not confirm the 118 million euro figure, saying it could only validate the order of magnitude.

## What is affected

The attacker used the bank's own account, not customers' accounts, to initiate transactions to other banking organizations. The bank said the incident had no impact on client assets and merely resulted in a delay in the execution of certain transfer transactions, and that it is now closed and under control.

## Why it matters

According to the report, the group has more than 5.8 million members and 211.5 billion euros in assets. The bank ruled out human error at the bank and described the incident as a sophisticated attack, and it ruled out Fortuneo as the entry point.

## What to do

The cited reporting names no affected version and no patch to apply, and the bank's response was to suspend certain systems. For customers, the action the report describes is vigilance: the bank warned of increased phishing risk and urged customers to stay vigilant against unsolicited communications.
