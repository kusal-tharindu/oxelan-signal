According to BleepingComputer, a threat actor breached the internal network of Korea Electric Power Corporation (KEPCO) and accessed two systems holding customer, employee, and contractor data. KEPCO is South Korea's main electric power company and a state-controlled operator of the country's critical infrastructure.

The company detected the attack on November 20, when it confirmed unauthorized access to the KEPCO ON electricity billing platform and the SRM electronic procurement system. The attack exposed details of more than 1.5 million individuals.

According to the report, the exposed KEPCO ON customer records cover 650,487 people and include names, ID information, phone numbers, addresses, bank names, bank account numbers, account holder names, card issuers, card numbers, cardholder names, and electricity charge and billing information. Records for 426,827 company representatives include names, ID information, email addresses, and company addresses. For 442,934 KEPCO employees, the exposed data includes names, affiliation, employee ID number, mobile phone number, email address, and company phone number.

## How the disclosure unfolded

A threat actor using the alias Noiem claimed the attack and advertised the breach on a hacker forum on November 19, saying they had KEPCO user data, supplier records, and bidding and contract documents. South Korea's Personal Information Protection Commission confirmed that KEPCO received an email from a hacker claiming data theft on November 19. The report says attribution to Noiem remains unconfirmed.

## Why it matters

The exposed records join identity details with bank account numbers, card numbers, and billing information. KEPCO says the breach resulted from advanced technologies such as AI, but it shared no specific details about the initial access method or the attack chain.

## What to do

KEPCO is notifying exposed individuals directly, has filed a report with the data protection authorities, and is coordinating with government and financial authorities to block abuse such as unauthorized transactions.
