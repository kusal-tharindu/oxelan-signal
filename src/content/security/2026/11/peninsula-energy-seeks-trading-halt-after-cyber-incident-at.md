Peninsula Energy requested a trading halt after a cybersecurity incident hit its uranium mine in the United States, according to SecurityWeek. The report said the incident occurred on Friday night and affected the Lance Project in Wyoming.

According to the report, the incident resulted in a breach of the company's \"operating system\", while administrative systems in the US and Australia were not affected. Peninsula Energy said the halt will remain in place until the earlier of the start of normal trading on November 25, 2026, or the release of its announcement about the cybersecurity incident.

## Why it matters

The Lance Project is wholly owned and sits in Crook County, where it relies on in situ recovery to extract uranium. In 2025, the US added uranium to the federal list of critical minerals.

Separately, the report said Dragos observed 19 ransomware attacks targeting mining organizations between July and September 2026. It also noted that AngloGold Ashanti disclosed in an SEC filing that it detected unauthorized activity on certain systems on October 16.
