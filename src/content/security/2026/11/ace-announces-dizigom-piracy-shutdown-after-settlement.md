## What changed

According to BleepingComputer, the Alliance for Creativity and Entertainment (ACE) announced the shutdown of Dizigom, one of Turkey's largest piracy sites, after a settlement agreement with the site's operators. Before being taken offline, the illegal TV and movie streaming service's websites had roughly 21 million visitors each month.

The report says the investigation into Dizigom's operations began three years ago, in 2023, with Turkish law enforcement authorities helping identify the platform's operators and their assets. Under the settlement, the operators agreed to cease operations, transfer control of 16 domains to ACE, and take the service offline.

## Why it matters

ACE reached the settlement agreement with the operators to secure the permanent shutdown of the service, according to the report. The domains that changed hands now redirect visitors to ACE's "Watch Legally" portal, where they can find out more about legitimate streaming sources. ACE is backed by more than 50 major television networks and film studios, including Disney, Warner Bros., Netflix, Paramount, Sony Pictures, and Universal Pictures.
