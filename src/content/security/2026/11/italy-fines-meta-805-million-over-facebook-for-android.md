According to SecurityWeek, Italy fined Meta Platforms Ireland Limited €805 million ($887 million) over a Facebook for Android gallery feature. The feature at issue is called "Get suggestions from gallery when browsing Facebook". Investigators found it was enabled by default and that Facebook requested gallery access through a single permission covering multiple app functions with different purposes.

## What is affected

The authority said the feature violated a European privacy directive incorporated into Italian law that requires free and informed consent for such access. Italy has about 20 million Android users, the agency said.

## Why it matters

The penalty makes the default setting and the bundled permission the center of the case. The authority said Meta's lack of cooperation with the investigation factored into the penalty, which amounts to approximately 0.47% of the group's global turnover.

## What to do

The authority gave Meta 60 days to disable the feature and seek separate consent for its use.
