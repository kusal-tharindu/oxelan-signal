## What happened

According to SecurityWeek, SAP is investigating a cybersecurity incident after reports that threat actors targeted its development infrastructure. The company said it had detected suspicious activity and implemented measures to protect customers and secure its services. SecurityWeek reported that Der Spiegel broke the news, citing SAP insiders and an internal notification sent to employees.

The outlet said an insider cited by Der Spiegel claimed the attackers obtained SAP employees' authentication tokens, enabling them to copy and alter source code stored on GitHub. It added that German news agency DPA reviewed internal SAP communications that revealed the suspicious activity had been identified within GitHub. Handelsblatt reported that SAP replaced signing keys, limited access to GitHub, and suspended automated development operations, according to SecurityWeek. The report added that sources cited by Der Spiegel suggested the attack may have been assisted by AI.

## What is affected

The reports concern SAP's development infrastructure and source code stored on GitHub. SAP says it found no evidence of unauthorized access to customer business data. None of the reports indicate that tampered code was incorporated into software delivered to SAP customers.

## What to do

Follow SAP's official communications for updates. The cited sources describe SAP's investigation and containment steps, not a customer-side fix or fixed versions.
