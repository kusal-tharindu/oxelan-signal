York University confirmed that an unauthorized actor accessed limited online forms used by University departments, and that personal information was stolen from some of those forms. The incident came to the University's attention on October 25, 2026. According to BleepingComputer, the unauthorized access affected forms submitted on 14 university websites between 2007 and 2026.

## What is affected

The incident affects individuals who completed online forms on the listed York University websites from the year set out for each site to October 2026. Most stolen information was basic information such as York identification numbers and contact information. The report says the university has not specified how many individuals have been impacted. It also says no details have been provided about the perpetrators, and that BleepingComputer did not find any ransomware group claiming the attack.

## Why it matters

York took immediate steps to contain the incident and has been working with external experts to investigate its scope and remediate the affected systems. The investigation has found no evidence that the unauthorized actor moved beyond the affected systems or gained access to other parts of the University's IT environment. York reported the privacy breach to the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Ontario and reported the incident to law enforcement.

## What to do

York will provide two years of complimentary TransUnion credit monitoring and identity theft protection to individuals whose government identification information or financial account information was exposed. The report said the university will directly notify individuals whose government identification or financial account information was exposed. Affected individuals should remain alert for suspicious emails, text messages and telephone calls, particularly communications asking for personal information. The report says the announcement describes the website forms as now secure and safe to use.
