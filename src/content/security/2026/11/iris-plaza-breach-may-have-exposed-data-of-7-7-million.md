According to SecurityWeek, a breach of an external cloud service may have exposed personal information of approximately 7.7 million people at Iris Plaza, the Japanese online shopping platform operated by Iris Ohyama. According to the report, the incident was discovered on October 28.

## What is affected

The report says 5.8 million current and former Iris Plaza members had names, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, dates of birth, gender information, and encrypted passwords compromised. The remaining 1.9 million individuals are non-members whose names, addresses, phone numbers, and email addresses were compromised. Iris Ohyama says the hacked external cloud service does not store credit card information.

## Why it matters

SecurityWeek describes the exposed passwords as encrypted and does not detail the encryption used. According to the report, there have been no reports of unauthorized use or financial damage resulting from the incident.

## What to do

Iris Ohyama says it has blocked the attackers' access to the service and notified law enforcement and the relevant authorities. The company has retained external experts to aid with the investigation. The Iris Plaza platform remains offline while the company investigates the cause of the incident and applies the necessary remediation measures. The report does not describe customer-facing remediation steps beyond those company statements.
