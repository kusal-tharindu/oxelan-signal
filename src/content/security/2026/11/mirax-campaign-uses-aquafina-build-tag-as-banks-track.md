According to Cyber Security News, the Mirax campaign uses the build tag aquafina, with the build-tag variant aquafina_all. The same report relays Group-IB guidance for banks: correlate Accessibility abuse, remote-control activity and screen sharing with transaction attempts, and hold suspicious payments for customer verification.

## Why it matters

For a bank, the recommendation joins device-control signals to the payment decision. The bank guidance is a correlation and payment-verification step for transaction attempts. Those device signals are listed alongside transaction attempts and payment holds, so the bank review covers more than the transaction amount. The cited material does not name a fixed app version or a vendor patch in the report.

## What to do

Users should reject unexpected Accessibility requests from entertainment apps. Users should avoid advertised APK (Android Package Kit) downloads. Banks should correlate Accessibility abuse, remote-control activity and screen sharing with transaction attempts. Banks should hold suspicious payments for customer verification. The report gives separate steps to users and to banks.
