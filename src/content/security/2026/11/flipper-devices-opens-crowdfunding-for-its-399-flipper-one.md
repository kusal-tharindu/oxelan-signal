According to BleepingComputer, Flipper Devices has launched a crowdfunding campaign for Flipper One, a portable Linux computer and networking multi-tool. The report says the device will launch at $399.

Flipper One runs a Debian-based system called Flipper OS; Debian is a Linux distribution, the open-source operating system family the device builds on. The report says the company states the device already runs on the mainline Linux kernel, the upstream kernel rather than a vendor fork. The same report lists built-in Wi-Fi 6E, Gigabit Ethernet, and an eSIM (an embedded SIM that joins a mobile network without a removable card).

The report says Flipper One can manage up to five network uplinks, act as a hotspot, run VPNs (virtual private networks) and firewalls, and connect to an external display over USB-C. It also describes the device as a separate product from Flipper Zero, focused on IP networking and on-device computing.

## Why it matters

The report sets the campaign between November 17 and December 17, with shipment for the first buyers estimated for July 2027. Flipper Devices used Kickstarter for Flipper Zero in 2020 and has since sold over 1 million devices, according to the report, which also says that campaign reached its funding goal within eight minutes and raised $4.8 million in 30 days.

## What to do

The cited report describes a new product and lists no affected versions, so there is no patch or mitigation to apply. The dates to work with are the campaign window between November 17 and December 17 and the shipment estimate of July 2027 for the first buyers.
