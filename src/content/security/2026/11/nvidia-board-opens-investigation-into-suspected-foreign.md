Nvidia's board of directors has announced an investigation into reports that chief executive officer (CEO) Jensen Huang was the target of a foreign intelligence effort. According to Cyber Security News, the review was announced on November 8, 2026.

The announcement followed Huang's acknowledgment to the board that he had been intimate with a business executive. In a letter to employees, Huang said he had been unfaithful to his wife and apologized to his family and colleagues, and said he took full responsibility for his actions.

The report also said that U.S. intelligence officials determined Alice Huang was gathering information for Beijing, citing The New York Times. Alice Huang was a Chinese citizen and chip buyer who previously worked as a television reporter in China before becoming a top executive at Singapore-based Megaspeed. Megaspeed bought more than $4.8 billion worth of Nvidia chips over the past two years.

## Why it matters

Nvidia said Huang, 63, will remain CEO during the investigation. Spokesperson John Rizzo said Huang is committed to continuing to lead Nvidia as CEO without interruption. The board said it has no reason to believe that either national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised.

## What to do

The cited sources name no affected version, fixed release or configuration change, so there is nothing to patch for this story. A committee of independent directors will oversee the inquiry with support from outside independent lawyers, and the board's statements are the record to follow.
