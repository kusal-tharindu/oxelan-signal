According to SecurityWeek, Turnitin has disclosed a cyberattack involving certain customer-facing systems. The report says chief executive officer (CEO) Chris Caren described the unauthorized activity in a November 23 letter on the company's website. Turnitin's own wording was that it "recently identified unauthorized activity involving certain customer-facing Turnitin systems".

Turnitin says those systems are fully operational and safe to use. The company also says the affected customer-facing systems do not collect government identification credentials or institutional financial information.

## Why it matters

Turnitin says its investigation into the nature and scope of the incident is ongoing. According to SecurityWeek, Turnitin did not say whether any data was accessed or how many customers might be affected. The report also said the University of Colorado Boulder was in touch with Turnitin to understand if any CU Boulder information was compromised.

## What to do

Follow Turnitin's updates on the investigation. The cited sources do not include fixed versions or a mitigation step, so there is no patch action to take from the disclosure alone.
