According to SecurityWeek, FinCEN is investigating a cybersecurity incident that resulted in unauthorized access to a limited subset of its systems. FinCEN is the US Treasury Department's financial intelligence unit. As a proactive measure, the agency has taken some systems offline.

## What is affected

The FinCEN notice was published on the Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) E-Filing System website. The BSA E-Filing System website is operational, but some FinCEN services may be temporarily unavailable.

## Why it matters

FinCEN has not shared further details on the nature of the incident and made no mention of a potential data breach. It is unclear who was behind the attack and if any data was accessed or stolen.

## What FinCEN has done

FinCEN extended the deadlines for qualifying filers affected by the disruption. The agency is granting temporary, limited exceptive relief in response to a service disruption hindering filers' ability to adhere to reporting obligations. The exceptive relief order was issued on November 24.
