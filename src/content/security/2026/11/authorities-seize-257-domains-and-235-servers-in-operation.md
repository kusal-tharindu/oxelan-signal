## What changed

According to BleepingComputer, authorities seized 257 domains and 235 servers in the operation. The report said the action is part of Operation Endgame, an international law enforcement effort targeting cybercrime and cybercrime-as-a-service networks. It said law enforcement agencies from Bulgaria, Canada, Denmark, Germany, Moldova, the Netherlands, Poland, the United Kingdom, and the United States took part, coordinated by Europol and Eurojust.

## Why it matters

The report said investigators identified 6.4 million victim datasets and over 6 million victim credentials on the targeted criminal infrastructure. It described Gootloader as a malware loader active since 2020, StealC as an infostealer that exfiltrates credentials, cryptocurrency wallets, and other sensitive information, and CastleRAT as a remote access trojan that surfaced last year. The report said victim data will be made available through platforms including HaveIBeenPwned, DIVD, CheckjeHack, NoMoreLeaks, Sekoia, The Shadowserver Foundation, and NL-NCSC.

The report said in June, Operation Endgame disrupted Amadey and StealC infrastructure, taking down 326 servers and 142 domains and recovering around 27 million credentials from more than 385,000 compromised systems. It said law enforcement also cleaned nearly 15,000 malware-infected WordPress websites and seized 106 servers used by the SocGholish botnet and Evil Corp.

## What to do

The report said Europol urged WordPress users to update credentials, enable multi-factor authentication, and patch vulnerabilities. Check whether your data appears on the victim-data platforms the report listed. If it does, update credentials and enable multi-factor authentication, and patch WordPress vulnerabilities as the report advised.
