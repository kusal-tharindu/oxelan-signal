Boston Scientific became aware of unauthorized access to its cloud infrastructure on May 12, according to BleepingComputer. The report says the intruder used voice phishing, or vishing, a phone-based form of social engineering, to gain access to the company's cloud infrastructure.

## What is affected

The breach exposed cloud-based applications and cloud-hosted data. Boston Scientific is a Massachusetts-based medical device maker, according to the report. The report puts its workforce at more than 59,000 employees and its presence in 127 countries.

## Why it matters

Boston Scientific disclosed a separate cyberattack on August 25, according to the report. That incident caused network outages and a global operational disruption, including to order processing and shipping. A spokesperson confirmed the most recent breach is unrelated to the August 2026 intrusion, the report says.

## What to do

The notification does not offer free credit monitoring to impacted individuals, the report says. It recommends using free credit reports and considering placing fraud alerts and credit freezes.
