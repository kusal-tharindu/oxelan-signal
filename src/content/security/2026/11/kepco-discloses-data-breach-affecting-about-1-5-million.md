According to SecurityWeek, KEPCO disclosed a data breach impacting approximately 1.5 million people. The report says the breach involved KEPCO ON, its integrated customer service portal, and the supplier relationship management (SRM) platform. According to the report, KEPCO confirmed on November 20 that data was exfiltrated from its systems. From KEPCO ON, the hackers stole the personal information of 650,487 people. The information of 869,761 individuals was stolen from the SRM systems.

## Why it matters

The reported theft covers personal information from both a customer service portal and a supplier platform. The report says a dark web forum user using the handle Noiem claimed to have stolen KEPCO user and supplier data. South Korea's Personal Information Protection Commission (PIPC) has not attributed the data breach to Noiem.

## What to do

The cited reporting does not list a fixed version or a product patch for KEPCO ON or the SRM platform. The response it describes is that KEPCO suspended the affected services immediately after discovering the incident and blocked external access. The PIPC launched an investigation into the incident.
