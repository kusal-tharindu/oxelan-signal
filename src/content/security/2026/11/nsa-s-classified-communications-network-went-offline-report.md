According to SecurityWeek, an internal network the National Security Agency (NSA) uses to communicate classified information was offline Tuesday. The report says the problem disrupted the operations of NSANet, a classified internal network used by employees of the agency. The outage was first reported by CNN.

## What is affected

The agency described the outage as due to routine maintenance and said it experienced temporary degradation during scheduled routine maintenance. The NSA said some internal systems were affected while mission-critical capabilities remain fully operational.

## Why it matters

Sen. Mark Warner said the evidence so far points toward legitimate efforts to secure systems, not malicious activity. Director of national intelligence Jay Clayton attributed the problem to government institutions and private companies pushing through upgrades of their computer systems around the same time. The report also noted that Apple acknowledged on its system status page that Apple Music, App Store and AppleCare services suffered full outages after an internal update.

## What to do

Top Trump administration national security officials said there was no indication the events were related or attributable to a cyberattack or hostile activity. Secretary of State Marco Rubio said there is zero evidence the incidents have anything to do with nefarious actors.
