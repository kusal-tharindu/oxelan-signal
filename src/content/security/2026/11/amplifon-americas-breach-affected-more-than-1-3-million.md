According to SecurityWeek, Amplifon Americas has disclosed a data breach affecting more than 1.3 million individuals in the United States. The publication describes the company as the Minneapolis-based US arm of the Italian hearing healthcare company Amplifon. The report says hackers broke into its internal systems in January 2026 and copied files from certain systems between January 18 and February 8.

Amplifon's filing with the Texas Attorney General's Office reportedly mentions a total of 1,332,826 people affected nationwide.

## What data was exposed

The stolen records included names, contact information, driver's license numbers, other government ID numbers, dates of birth, hearing and medical information, dates of service, health information, and health insurance information. For some individuals, Social Security numbers and financial information were also stolen.

## Why it matters

Because the same record set combines government identifiers, dates of birth and health information, the exposure goes beyond contact details. The report says Amplifon needed eight months to review the stolen data before determining that personal information was potentially affected.

## What to do

Anyone assessing their own exposure can use the data categories in the disclosure, since Social Security numbers and financial information apply only to some of the affected individuals. The company said it is not aware of any fraudulent use of personal information as a result of the incident.
