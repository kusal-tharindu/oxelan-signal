According to SecurityWeek, Microsoft used its Ignite 2026 conference on Tuesday to announce the general availability of Perception, a product designed for coordinating teams of artificial intelligence (AI) agents to handle security tasks. The report says the system was unveiled in July as Project Perception and has been in public preview inside Microsoft Defender since August.

## What is generally available

According to SecurityWeek, four blue team agents are now generally available: alert triage, attack investigation, detection authoring and threat intelligence. The report says the Recon Agent, one of the red team agents, is generally available too, as is the Posture Prioritization agent, and that two new green team playbooks work across Microsoft, AWS (Amazon Web Services) and Google Cloud.

## What stays in preview

The report says a Web App Pen Test Agent has entered preview, and that the Risk Reduction agent is in public preview and can recommend vulnerability shielding when a fix is not immediately available. According to SecurityWeek, Microsoft is also previewing integrations that link Perception to SIEM (security information and event management), SOAR (security orchestration, automation and response) and IT service management tools via MCP (Model Context Protocol) and APIs. The report adds that, in preview, Agent 365 and Entra Suite can detect 60 types of local AI agents, including Claude Code, OpenAI Codex and GitHub Copilot.

## Why it matters

Aleš Holeček, president and chief architect of Microsoft Security, wrote in a blog post that the customer's team sets the guardrails and signs off on high-impact actions, and that automated triggers launch playbooks when an incident meets the criteria the customer sets, the report says. Microsoft's claim that the Alert Triage Agent can handle roughly 80% of alerts in a typical Defender queue rests on an analysis of approximately 8 million alerts across its security products, according to the report.
