## What Maersk confirmed

Maersk said on November 11, 2026 that it had identified an IT security incident involving unauthorized access and data extraction from one of its local legacy systems from a previous acquisition. The company said its findings so far strongly suggest no sensitive data has been disclosed. Maersk said the incident is isolated to a local system, with no indication of impact on other Maersk systems or on customer and partner systems that connect through integrations such as APIs and EDI (electronic data interchange).

## What the extortion group claims

According to BleepingComputer, the WhiteArmor ransomware group listed Maersk on its data leak site and publicly asked for a ransom payment of $5 million. The report says the group gave Maersk 30 days to pay USD 5,000,000 in bitcoin, and claims to hold approximately 100 GB of data allegedly stolen from Maersk's OCF/LF Logistics platform. According to the report, Maersk acquired LF Logistics, the original developer of that platform, from the Li & Fung Group in 2022.

According to BleepingComputer, WhiteArmor claims the data includes nearly 30,000 driver records, plaintext passwords, 33 SUPER_ADMIN accounts, the most privileged accounts on a system, signed delivery receipts, and inventory rows with purchase orders and Advance Shipping Notices (ASNs). The report says the group also claims 27,842 mobile device logs containing phone numbers, emails, and IMEI (International Mobile Equipment Identity) numbers.

## Why it matters

Maersk is a large organization with a documented ransomware history. According to BleepingComputer, Maersk was hit by NotPetya ransomware in 2017, which forced it to rebuild over 4,000 servers and 45,000 workstations from scratch. The report says the company operated without IT for ten days and incurred damages of up to $300 million.

## What to do

Maersk asked recipients to treat any unexpected communications claiming to relate to this matter with caution and to verify them through their usual Maersk contact. The company said customers can continue to use Maersk platforms to book and manage their shipments as usual, and that it does not anticipate disruption to operations for customers at this time.
