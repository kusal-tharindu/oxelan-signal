According to BleepingComputer, Turnitin detected unauthorized activity involving certain customer-facing systems and implemented its incident response plan. The company notified law enforcement and cyber regulators, the report says. Turnitin says its investigation remains ongoing and has not disclosed details about the nature and scope of the unauthorized activity.

Turnitin says its systems are fully operational and safe to use. The report notes that the company has not disclosed whether any user data was accessed or stolen. No ransomware groups have publicly claimed responsibility for the attack, according to the report.

The report notes that Turnitin software is used in over 16,000 institutions across 185 countries and serves 71 million students. The cited report gives no fixed version or mitigation step, and Turnitin has not described the nature or scope of the activity.

## What to do

Review Turnitin's statements and monitor for updates; the cited report gives no fixed version or mitigation step. The report does not say whether user data was accessed or stolen.
