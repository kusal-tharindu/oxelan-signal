Corma has raised $125 million in a Series A funding round, according to SecurityWeek. The round was led by NEA, with previous backers Sequoia Capital, Khosla Ventures and Coatue also participating, the report says. The new money brings Corma's total funding to $185 million and its valuation to $1 billion.

## What Corma builds

Corma was founded in 2025 and is headquartered in Tel Aviv and San Francisco. It emerged from stealth mode in August with a frontier foundation model specifically designed for defensive cybersecurity. The company trains frontier foundation models and deploys them as AI (artificial intelligence) agents alongside existing security tools.

## Why it matters

Corma's pitch is a model trained specifically for defensive cybersecurity rather than a general-purpose one. The round, and the $1 billion valuation attached to it, show how investors are pricing that specific application of foundation models.

## What the report does not cover

SecurityWeek's report does not describe pricing, availability, or which existing security tools the agents are deployed alongside. It also does not break out the earlier funding that makes up the $185 million total.
