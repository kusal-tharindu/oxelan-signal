## What happened

According to BleepingComputer, Ukrainian law enforcement arrested four suspects in a cryptocurrency investment scam case involving the theft of more than €4.3 million. The report says the ring targeted potential victims across the European Union and took the money from 135 German victims. Ukrainian police detained four members, including the leader and three accomplices, after two rounds of searches in the Kyiv, Vinnytsia, Zaporizhzhia, and Ivano-Frankivsk regions.

## How the scheme worked

According to the report, the suspects used phone calls and messaging apps to build trust through social engineering and steer victims into cryptocurrency, shares, and other financial instruments on fraudulent online trading platforms. They also helped victims open accounts on cryptocurrency exchanges, buy digital assets, and move them to accounts controlled by the criminal organization; in some cases, the report says, they gained access to victims' online banking accounts. Proceeds moved through bank accounts, cryptocurrency exchanges, and transit wallets before being converted and distributed among members of the organization.

## Investigation and charges

The first stage of the operation took place in August 2026 with 39 searches; a second phase in September 2026 added 17 searches in Kyiv and the Kyiv region. It was carried out by Ukraine's Cyber Police Department and the Main Investigation Department under the procedural supervision of the Office of the Prosecutor General, together with German law enforcement. A Joint Investigation Team (JIT) was established between Ukrainian and German authorities to coordinate the work.

The four detainees and one additional accomplice were notified of suspicion under Part 4 of Article 28, Part 4 of Article 190, and Parts 1 and 2 of Article 255 of the Criminal Code of Ukraine, covering fraud and participation in a criminal organization. The offences are punishable by up to 12 years' imprisonment with confiscation of property. A court ordered the organizer and three accomplices held in pre-trial detention and placed another alleged co-organizer under house arrest. The pre-trial investigation is ongoing, and officers are still working to identify everyone involved and additional victims.
