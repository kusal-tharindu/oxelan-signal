According to BleepingComputer, LaLiga disclosed a data breach that exposed personal information belonging to users of its services and those of some affiliated clubs. The report says a cybercriminal gained unauthorized access to a fan-services platform, where the attacker extracted personal data belonging to users registered for digital services offered by LaLiga and some clubs, in an incident that occurred between November 14 and 16.

## What is affected

LaLiga said the incident was confined to a specific platform and did not extend to other systems that support the running of the competitions. On November 16, the organization's security team identified the source of the unauthorized access and implemented measures to block the attack and stop the data extraction.

## Why it matters

The records involved belong to users registered for digital services offered by the league and by some clubs. At the time of writing, no ransomware group had claimed responsibility for the attack, the report said.

## What to do

LaLiga reported the incident to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD, the national data protection regulator) and filed a complaint with the National Police's Central Cybercrime Unit. Impacted clubs were directly notified, and actions to inform impacted fans are being coordinated. The cited report describes no fixed version or software patch for users, since the documented response is containment of the unauthorized access and notification. Administrators of affected digital services should wait for that coordinated notification before treating the matter as closed.
