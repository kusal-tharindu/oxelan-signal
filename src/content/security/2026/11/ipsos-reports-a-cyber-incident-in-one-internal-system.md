## What Ipsos disclosed

Ipsos said it identified a cybersecurity incident affecting one internal information system and filed a complaint with the relevant authorities. According to SecurityWeek, the market research firm made the incident public on Monday, November 16, five days after filing that complaint.

## What is known about the scope

Ipsos said the investigation is ongoing to determine the nature and scope of the incident. According to SecurityWeek, the company has not said whether any data was accessed or stolen, and no known cybercrime group appears to have taken credit for an attack on Ipsos.

## Why it matters

Ipsos said the incident had no impact on its operations or its ability to deliver for its clients. According to SecurityWeek, the firm was established in France in 1975 and has nearly 20,000 employees across 90 markets and 5,000 clients. That makes the unanswered question about data access the part that matters to those clients and partners.

## Response so far

Ipsos said it activated its incident response procedures, took steps to contain the incident, and engaged external cybersecurity specialists to support the investigation. The company said it would contact affected clients, projects or business partners directly if the investigation establishes that their information was affected.

## What to do

Clients and business partners should watch for direct contact from Ipsos, which said it would reach those concerned directly if the investigation shows their information was affected. The cited sources do not list affected versions or fixed versions to apply, and Ipsos describes the incident as affecting one internal information system. The investigation to determine the nature and scope of the incident is ongoing.
