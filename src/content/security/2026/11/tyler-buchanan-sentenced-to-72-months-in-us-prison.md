Tyler Robert Buchanan, 24, of Dundee, Scotland, was sentenced to 72 months in federal prison. According to BleepingComputer, Buchanan is a leader of the Scattered Spider cybercrime collective. He pleaded guilty on April 17 to one count of conspiracy to commit wire fraud and one count of aggravated identity theft.

The conspiracy ran from September 2021 to April 2023 and involved hacking into at least a dozen companies' computer systems through text message phishing attacks. The scheme involved the theft of at least $11.6 million worth of virtual currency from individual victims in the United States. The report said the conspirators compromised victims' cryptocurrency accounts and wallets via SIM (Subscriber Identity Module) swaps.

Buchanan has been in federal custody since April 2025, and he is the fifth and final defendant to be sentenced in the case. A restitution hearing is scheduled for December 11. The report said Buchanan was arrested in Palma de Mallorca, Spain, in June 2024.

The other defendants include Ahmed Hossam Eldin Elbadawy, 25, of College Station, Texas, sentenced to 45 months; Evans Onyeaka Osiebo, 22, of Dallas, Texas, sentenced to 45 months; Noah Michael Urban, serving a 10-year federal prison sentence; and Joel Martin Evans, 27, of Jacksonville, North Carolina, serving a two-year federal prison sentence. The Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) investigated the matter.

The report said Scattered Spider is also tracked as 0ktapus, Scatter Swine, Octo Tempest, Starfraud, UNC3944, and Muddled Libra. It said the group has partnered with Russian ransomware operations including BlackCat/AlphV, Qilin, and RansomHub since early 2023.
