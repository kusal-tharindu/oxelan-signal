# Privacy

**Last updated:** 2026-10-27
**Contact:** hello@oxelan.com

Oxelan Signal is a static publication. It has no accounts, no login, no
newsletter tracker, no advertising cookies and no analytics by default.

## What is collected

- **Reading the site.** The site itself sets no cookies. GitHub Pages (and, when
  proxied, Cloudflare) may process request metadata such as IP address, user
  agent, referrer and timestamps to deliver the site, prevent abuse and keep
  service logs.
- **Email.** If you email a correction, tip or permission request, your address
  and message are used to respond and keep a record of the correction.
- **GitHub issues.** Correction and security reports opened on GitHub are public
  by design. Do not include confidential information, personal data or exploit
  details in a public issue.
- **RSS.** Feeds are fetched directly by your reader. The site does not track
  subscribers or embed tracking pixels in feeds.
- **Analytics.** The repository default is off. If analytics is enabled later,
  it will be cookieless, aggregate and disclosed on this page and in the site
  configuration. The CSP blocks third-party analytics scripts unless the feature
  flag and token are set.

## What is not done

- No selling or renting of personal data.
- No advertising profiles, cross-site tracking or affiliate tracking.
- No account creation or password storage.
- No collection of payment data (there is no payment feature).

## Retention

Hosting and CDN logs are retained by GitHub and Cloudflare under their own
policies. Email is retained only as long as needed to handle the request and keep
a correction record. Public issues remain public until deleted by the repository
owner or GitHub.

## Your choices

Contact **hello@oxelan.com** to ask what personal data is held about you, to
request deletion where applicable, or to ask a privacy question. If you are in a
jurisdiction with specific rights, state that in the message.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be dated here and, where appropriate,
noted on the About page.
